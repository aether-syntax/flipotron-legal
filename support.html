How to play

Open How to Play from the home screen for a step-by-step demo on a real board. In short:

  • Take turns placing a token on an empty square of the 7×7 board.
  • Trap a line of your opponent's tokens between two of yours — across, down or diagonally — and they flip to your side.
  • The four pulse tiles in the middle can't be played on and block captures. After every third placement they pulse, pushing neighbouring tokens one step away.
  • A match is exactly 24 placements (45 in Full Board mode). Whoever has more tokens at the end wins.

Common questions

Play Nearby can't find my friend. Both devices need Flipotron open on the Play Nearby screen, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on, and permission for Local Network (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or Nearby devices (Android). Keep the devices close together. Connecting can take up to a minute. Play Nearby works between two Apple devices or between two Android devices.

My purchase didn't unlock. Open the Store screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account.

Online play. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, sign in to Game Center in Settings to play online.

Delete my data. All game data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
